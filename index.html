A Self-Evolving Data Engine with Parallel Worlds Expansion
for Scalable Robot Learning

Existing data generation methods for robot learning suffer from limited exploration, embodiment gaps, low signal-to-noise ratios, and domain shifts, leading to performance degradation during self-iteration and poor generalization to unseen scenes. We propose Seed2Scale, a self-evolving data engine with parallel worlds expansion. Starting with as few as four seed demonstrations, Seed2Scale first executes a self-evolution stage driven by a heterogeneous synergy of "small-model collection, large-model evaluation, and target-model learning", then projects self-evolved trajectories into parallel worlds for visual generalization — achieving a 75.38% success rate in zero-shot real-world evaluations, where baseline methods fail completely (0%).

01 — Abstract

Breaking the
Data Bottleneck

A dual-stage self-evolving framework that generates massive, high-quality robot trajectories from minimal human input — and projects them into parallel worlds for real-world transfer.

The emergence of Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models is accelerating the evolution of Embodied AI. However, the performance of these models remains strictly contingent upon large-scale, high-quality expert demonstration trajectories, creating a critical "data scarcity" bottleneck that fundamentally obstructs the scaling of robot learning.

To address this, we propose Seed2Scale, a self-evolving data engine with parallel worlds expansion, operating through a dual-stage pipeline. In the self-evolution stage, a lightweight SuperTiny collector (πsmall) enables robust exploration from only 4 seed trajectories, a frozen Qwen3-VL verifier (ΦVLV) autonomously scores and filters trajectories to prevent model collapse, and a SmolVLA target model (πtarget) learns exclusively from curated trajectories. In the parallel worlds stage, self-evolved trajectories are projected into diverse environments of the same task, enhancing adaptability to unseen scenes and real-world deployment.

Experiments demonstrate consistent scaling as iterations progress. The self-evolution stage lifts Seen-scene success from 22.18% to 68.57%, while Parallel Worlds raises Unseen-scene success from 0% to 65.34%. Notably, Seed2Scale achieves a 75.38% success rate in zero-shot real-world evaluations, where baseline methods fail completely (0%). Code, datasets, assets, and model weights will be open-sourced.

03 — Framework

The Seed2Scale Pipeline

From 4 boundary-point demonstrations to massive high-quality training data — a dual-stage self-evolving ecosystem combining self-evolution with parallel worlds expansion.

Fig. 1 — The Seed2Scale self-evolving data engine. Starting with as few as four human demonstrations, the framework utilizes a lightweight SuperTiny VLA model for large-scale parallel data collection. A pretrained Vision-Language Model acts as a Verifier, autonomously scoring and filtering the raw trajectories to prevent model collapse. A parallel-world projection mechanism then achieves visual generalization on top of the position generalization accomplished through self-evolution, yielding large-scale, high-quality, and diverse datasets for model training.
04 — Methodology

Heterogeneous Synergy + Parallel Worlds

Three specialized model roles and one trajectory-level augmentation stage, each exploiting distinct scale asymmetries to achieve what no single model can accomplish alone.

SuperTiny Collector

A lightweight 48M-parameter VLA model: a ResNet-18 vision encoder, frozen T5-Small language encoder, and a compact MLP state embedding are unified into a conditioning memory, processed by a query-based Transformer decoder with action chunking (K=30) and temporal ensembling. Achieves 26.3 Hz inference — 3.6× faster than Diffusion Policy — and bootstraps robustly from just 4 seed trajectories.

πsmall · 48M params · 26.3 Hz

VLM-as-a-Verifier

A two-stage verification pipeline: a rule-based success gate filters failed trajectories by design, then a frozen Qwen3-VL-32B serves as the Vision-Language Verifier (VLV), scoring execution quality from the task instruction, rollout video, and a reference demo. Only trajectories with Sτ ≥ γ enter Dsilver, preventing data-poisoning spirals.

ΦVLV · Qwen3-VL-32B · Frozen

Parallel Worlds

A trajectory-conditioned stage that projects self-evolved trajectories into diverse target worlds of the same task — with task-relevant objects at source-aligned poses but varied assets, backgrounds, and cameras. Feasibility checks (workspace, kinematics, collision, contact, smoothness) precede re-collection, and VLV-filtered rollouts augment the final dataset.

Trajectory projection · 10 worlds · 3DGS

SmolVLA Target Model

The final policy (πtarget) trained via Conditional Flow Matching on the curated dataset Dfinal. Its Action Expert architecture interleaves Cross-Attention (CA) over vision-language context with Self-Attention (SA) for temporal coherence — enabling stable closed-loop deployment on Agibot A2 and GR-1 robots.

πtarget · 450M params · Flow Matching

08 — Multi-Task Demo

Four-Task Generalization

Multi-task joint training with cross-task knowledge transfer consistently outperforms single-task training. Evaluated on Seen scenes (10 parallel worlds) and Unseen scenes (3 disjoint worlds) on the Agibot A2 platform.

Kitchen Cleanup

Pick up the chip from the table, then place it into the basket. Tests precise grasping and placement from arbitrary positions beyond the 4 seed locations.

Seen: 68.50% · Unseen: 75.51%

Cup-to-Cup Transfer

Pour berries from the cup on the right into the cup on the left. Requires fine motor control and understanding of granular transfer dynamics with non-uniform objects.

Seen: 60.61% · Unseen: 61.62%

Can Stacking

Stack cans from both sides into the center and ensure they are placed stably. The hardest task for the 4-seed baseline, requiring complex spatial reasoning far beyond the seed demos.

Seen: 67.70% · Unseen: 69.19%

Air Fryer Manipulation

Grasp carrots from a plate, then open the air fryer and place the food inside. Tests multi-step manipulation with articulated objects, requiring precise door-handle grasping and coordinated placement.

Seen: 71.21% · Unseen: 55.05%
